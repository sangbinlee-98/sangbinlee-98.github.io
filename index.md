# Sangbin Lee (이상빈)

## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Architecture and Architectural Engineering at Seoul National University. My research focuses on structural health monitoring, data-driven structural analysis, and AI-based methods for civil infrastructure systems.

## Research Interests

- Structural Health Monitoring
- Physics-informed Neural Operators
- Data-driven Structural Analysis

## Current Research

My current research explores data-driven and physics-informed approaches for understanding, monitoring, and predicting the behaviour of civil infrastructure systems.
In particular, I am interested in combining structural mechanics, sensing data, and artificial intelligence to support more reliable infrastructure monitoring and decision-making.
